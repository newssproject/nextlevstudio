FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project.

What type of videos do you edit? +

I edit different types of content, including TikTok, Reels, YouTube videos, promotional content, personal videos, and more.

Can I request a specific editing style? +

Of course! You can send me a reference or explain the style you're looking for, and I'll edit the video accordingly.

How do I send my footage? +

You can send your footage through Google Drive, Dropbox, or any convenient file-sharing platform.

How long does the editing take? +

Turnaround time depends on the length and complexity of the video. I'll let you know the estimated delivery time when you book.

Can I request changes after receiving the video? +

Yes! Minor amendments are welcome. Any major changes or additional work outside the original request may be charged separately.
